# Typed review policy validation matrix

**Status: READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION — NOT_ADOPTED.** This is a manual, specification-level re-evaluation of the revised amendment in `08-review-policy-scope-repair.md`, performed against the current `AGENTS.md` and the research lifecycle contract. It is not an independent validation, an implementation review, an adoption decision, or authorization to dispatch reviewers. No reviewer was dispatched and no policy validator was found.

## Method and observed behavior

- Current `AGENTS.md` line 11 requires `native_lifecycle.py prepare-review` before **any** reviewer dispatch, and says a failed check blocks all reviewer dispatch. It then requires `validate-review` for reviewer handoffs. This is the controlling current rule while the amendment remains proposed.
- The research contract in `.agents/skills/crypto-mcp-research/references/agent-handoffs.md` defines `prepare-review` as a research-lifecycle supervisor checkpoint. It checks terminal specialist receipts, handoffs, closure, reconciliation, frozen snapshot/digest and related lifecycle evidence; its output is not a runtime authorization boundary.
- `scripts/native_lifecycle.py` implements `prepare-review` and `validate-review`; `tests/test_native_lifecycle.py` exercises fail-closed lifecycle conditions and binding checks. These validate research lifecycle behavior, not `RESEARCH_REVIEW`/`ENGINEERING_REVIEW` classification.
- Repository search found no executable typed-review-policy validator or typed-classification tests. Therefore the observed results below are manual rule application, not automated enforcement or runtime observations. For reproducibility, the inputs are the two policy files and the lifecycle contract named above; the outcome for a case is conditional only where expressly stated. “Observed proposed outcome” means the outcome obtained by manually applying the revised text to the stated case facts; it does not mean a scenario was executed by a tool.

“Current dispatch” applies the current global gate as written. Where a case has no successful `prepare-review` result in its stated facts, dispatch is denied; where the table says it passed, current policy permits dispatch after that gate, without implying that current policy validates engineering-specific evidence or mixed scope.

## Revalidation of identified wording findings

| Finding mapped from first pass | Scenario(s) | Revised wording and revalidated result |
|---|---|---|
| Policy-self-review had no stable class; policy validation could be mistaken for review dispatch or adoption. | 11, 12 | `POLICY_DECISION` is explicitly outside the two reviewer dispatch types. Validation is non-dispatch; implementation review cannot authorize policy change; only authorized explicit acceptance does. Scenario 11 has one result: no reviewer dispatch and no adoption. Scenario 12 splits code correctness from authority change and denies bypass. |
| Reviewer independence lacked an auditable criterion. | 1, 3, 5, 6, 9 | Record identity, role, contribution and conflict disclosures plus disposition. Authors/implementers/material contributors to target are ineligible; conflicts reasonably affecting impartiality disqualify. Scenario 9's participating implementer is explicitly ineligible. |
| Mixed request and research gate relabeling could be split opportunistically. | 3, 4, 6 | Mixed decisions require separate recorded scopes and outcomes. Relabeling or artifact/subtarget carving cannot convert the same research decision. A genuine implementation-only decision must be independently meaningful, expressly exclude research judgments, and never substitute for or be represented as the blocked research decision. |
| Ambiguous requests had no terminal outcome. | 10 | `UNRESOLVED` means no dispatch until target and decision are clarified, recorded, and classified. |
| Code implementing reviewer controls versus authority changes was not distinguished. | 1, 12 | Testing correctness against current policy is engineering review. Changing dispatch authority or removing a gate is `POLICY_DECISION`; engineering approval cannot waive the current gate or authorize the change. |
| Current policy lacks the proposed typed safeguards. | 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 | This remains a known current-policy limitation, not a change made by this draft. Until acceptance/integration, current `AGENTS.md` still imposes the global prepare-review gate and does not encode the new engineering evidence/classification rules. |

## Verdict

**READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION — NOT_ADOPTED.** Manual re-evaluation assigns an explicit classification or no-dispatch result and required prerequisites to all 12 cases. No unresolved wording disagreement remains in those scenarios. Remaining limitations are explicit: the classifier is not executable, procedural wording is not runtime enforcement, independent validation has not occurred, acceptance has not been granted, and current `AGENTS.md` remains controlling. This report does not authorize adoption, reviewer dispatch, or changes to current policy.
